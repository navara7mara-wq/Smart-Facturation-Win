# User Acceptance Checklist

Use with a business owner after technical audit. Items marked with confirmation require explicit domain acceptance.

- [ ] First launch and mandatory admin password replacement are understandable.
- [ ] Company, branches, clients and client directions match the real organization.
- [ ] BPU General/ST content and mapping are approved by the contract owner.
- [ ] All purchase-order natures and partner requirements match actual practice.
- [ ] Invoice numbering and fiscal-year rules are approved. **BUSINESS RULE REQUIRES CONFIRMATION**
- [ ] VAT, retention, rounding and total formulas are approved. **BUSINESS RULE REQUIRES CONFIRMATION**
- [ ] Draft-to-paid statuses and authorized corrections match operational roles.
- [ ] Dashboard KPI definitions and overdue threshold are approved. **BUSINESS RULE REQUIRES CONFIRMATION**
- [ ] Invoice, quantitative quotation and estimate layouts meet legal/contractual requirements. **BUSINESS RULE REQUIRES CONFIRMATION**
- [ ] French and Arabic customer/site names render correctly in UI, XLSX and PDF.
- [ ] Backup/restore steps are understandable and recover known data.
- [ ] Licence activation and DEMO limits match commercial terms.
- [ ] Installer, shortcuts, first launch, restart, update and uninstall are accepted.
- [ ] Keyboard navigation and focus are acceptable for daily use.
